# Gamebop

Free games you can play right in your browser, on a laptop or a phone. No downloads, no sign-up.

**▶ Play now: https://charlieshocked-a11y.github.io/gamebop/**

![Gamebop](img/share.jpg)

- Racing, puzzles, typing games, sports, platformers and more
- New games every Sunday
- Leaderboards and a phone settings bar (volume, swap and move the touch buttons)
- Your progress saves in your browser
